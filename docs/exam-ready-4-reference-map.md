## Information hierarchy

Primary: current lesson/question and one continue/submit action. Secondary: stage, source provenance, elapsed/study time. Contextual: explanation depth, hints, worked solution, mistake repair and materials. Optional: notes, formula dock, scratchpad, history and replan controls in drawers/details. No utility is removed solely to simplify the main screen.

Command center: setup, overview, materials and final review. Classroom: learn, practice, recall and repair, all around the real current mission. Viewing another activity or an already saved lesson must not silently mark a future checkpoint complete, reset timers or regenerate AI context.

Liquid Glass: scoped navy/cyan/violet translucent gradients, fine specular edges, limited blur, pointer highlights and compositor-friendly entry/selection transitions. Calm reading surfaces; stronger cosmic framing for welcome/build. No reference screenshot is installed as a fake interface/background.
