## 4. Groq rate-limit findings

Groq documents organization-level quotas, HTTP 429 and retry-after headers; account quota is not inferred from generic documentation. The new real 429 provided a 30-second wait. It was preserved as metadata and halted the batch. Numeric seconds and HTTP-date parsing have deterministic coverage, including a two-day header without shortening its wait. No credential/provider/model rotation or background sleep-and-retry. [Groq rate limits](https://console.groq.com/docs/rate-limits).

## 14. Lesson explanation depth

## 15. Context-aware teaching

The prompt now includes bounded current lesson, concept, worked example, unanswered check, previous answer and actual current-source versions. Questions sent as pending context omit keys. Unrelated chapter attempts and stale/deleted/excluded source context are excluded. Learner notes are context rather than factual authority unless the permitted notepad is explicitly retrieved.

## 16. Conversational interruption handling

Current task and question remain attached to follow-ups; staged core adaptation can retain an unanswered bank check without treating it as a repeated evaluated question. Explicit hints/solutions operate on that permitted current question. Broad new conversational interpretation still depends on working live service. Guest arbitrary questions return clearly labeled source guidance, not a fake personalized answer.

## 17. Question difficulty improvements

One easy correct answer no longer immediately selects advanced content. Advanced evidence needs multiple unassisted attempts including application; explicit expert requests select level five. Equal-distance bank ties now prefer a suitable challenge rather than an easier question. The final actual strong-confidence Maths browser journey changed from a simple coefficient calculation to a parameter-plus-convolution problem.

## 18. Challenging question examples

For integer n≥3, C(n,2)/C(n,1)=7/2 gives n=8; degree-three coefficient of (1+x)^8(1−2x)^3 is −24. A second challenge with a>0 and equal x²/x³ coefficients in (1+ax)^8 gives a=1/2; degree-three coefficient after multiplying by (1−2x)^5 is 17. Both require constraint inference and all signed cross terms, not just a middle-term lookup relabeled hard.

## 19. Independently checked solutions

Independent coefficient-vector multiplication gives 56−168+96−8=−24 and 7−70+160−80=17. First-principles differentiation checks expansion/cancellation before taking the limit, not division at h=0. Redox check conserves both atoms and total charge. Existing numerical fixtures are recalculated; LAM is not asked to grade its own output as the sole verifier.

## 20. Hint progression

Three source-owned or derived hints move from stated conditions to method to setup/intermediate calculation; full solution is explicitly requested. Hints preserve the current check and do not dump its entire solution. Assistance metadata survives subsequent support turns. Legacy generic hints are useful scaffolding but not a complete individualized misconception engine.

## 21. Answer-scoring improvements

Numerical working embedded in a response is sent to rubric assessment rather than incorrectly treated as a unit suffix. Correct bare polynomial conclusions can earn conclusion-only credit without invented method marks. Bounded one-variable polynomial equivalence handles degree ≤8, limited tokens/depth and constant denominators; unknown functions, variable denominators, executable syntax and larger expressions are unsupported, not approximated by point sampling.

## 22. Misconception repair

First-law transfer now has fresh questions after a wrong existing first-law answer. Original depth units include explicit wrong claim, repair, diagnostic and transfer. Sign errors and unit errors are distinguished when demonstrated; a wrong number alone does not prove a specific arithmetic versus method error. Guest MCQ feedback is factual self-review and does not become evaluated server evidence.

## 23. Mastery-evidence handling

Assisted and Guest self-reviewed answers do not count as independent readiness. Demonstrated objectives require a successful application plus a distinct-pattern later unassisted retrieval/mock response after at least 15 minutes; retainedAcrossDays is separate. Same numeric-pattern variants and back-to-back recall are not retention proof. Untested objectives and whole-chapter coverage gaps remain visible.
